# Turns

A band takes one place in the initiative order, and the turn moves through it by itself.

## How the turn moves

1. The tracker stops on the first member of the band.
2. The GM passes the turn. Minion! steps through each remaining member of the band, one after the other, with each member's own start and end of turn.
3. After the last member, the turn stops on the next creature in the order.

**Previous turn** goes back to the first member of the band.

## What to expect

- While the turn steps through a band, the reaction marker of the other members stays visible.
- Start-of-turn effects of a member fire as the turn reaches that member.
- **Previous turn** skips start and end of turn effects.

If the turn can't go through every member, the GM gets a notification and the console has the details.

## Order in the tracker

The order is recalculated on every client after each change to the groups, so everyone sees the same order.
