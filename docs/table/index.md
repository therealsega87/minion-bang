How Minion! behaves during play, in the order a fight uses it.

1. [The Minion trait](minion-trait.md): what takes a minion down.

Everything on these pages needs [Midi-QOL](https://foundryvtt.com/packages/midi-qol): Minion! reads the damage it calculates. Players keep attacking the usual way, with no extra button.
