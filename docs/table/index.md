# At the table

How Minion! behaves during play, in the order a fight runs into it:

1. [The Minion trait](minion-trait.md): what takes a minion down.
2. [Overkill](overkill.md): what a big hit does to the minions around its target.
3. [Shared turns and bands](../bands/index.md): how a horde fits into the tracker.
4. [What happens when](edge-cases.md): unusual situations, one line each.

All of it needs [Midi-QOL](https://foundryvtt.com/packages/midi-qol), because Minion! works from the damage Midi-QOL calculates. Players attack as usual, with no extra button.
