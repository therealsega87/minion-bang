# What happens when

Unusual situations, one line each.

## Attacks and damage

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| An attack hits a minion for 1 damage | It drops. |
| A minion succeeds on its saving throw and the damage is below its maximum | Nothing. It takes no damage at all. |
| A minion succeeds on its saving throw and the damage reaches its maximum | It drops. |
| A minion is immune to the damage | It takes none and stays up. |
| A monster or an NPC attacks | The Minion trait applies. No overkill. |
| A spell attack hits | The Minion trait applies. No overkill. |
| One attack hits several targets | Each target is handled on its own, and none of them is offered as an extra minion. |
| A minion already at 0 hit points is hit again | No new death is recorded, but the hit can still start an overkill. |
| Someone edits a minion's hit points by hand | The Minion trait doesn't apply. |

## Overkill

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| The attacking player isn't connected | The GM gets the dialog. |
| The GM is looking at another scene | The minion hit drops. No overkill is offered, and the GM gets a notification. |
| Nobody answers | The dialog and the GM's waiting window stay open until someone answers. |
| Someone closes the dialog | It counts as **Cancel**. The chat says no overkill happened. |
| Several overkills happen at once | Their dialogs open one after another. |
| The player answers **Yes (no overkill)** | Nothing more falls once they click **Confirm**. No chat message. |
| The player clicks **Confirm** with no minion checked | No overkill. The chat says so. |
| An extra minion can't be downed | The others fall. The GM sees which ones failed. |

## Bands

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| A minion has no challenge rating | It's never put in a band. |
| One minion of a kind joins before anyone rolls initiative | It stays alone until another minion of its kind joins. |
| The captain drops or leaves the combat | The first member still standing becomes captain. The band keeps its initiative. |
| A member rolls after the band has its initiative | The band's initiative doesn't change. |
| The GM resets initiative | The bands lose their initiative too. |
| A minion joins after someone has rolled initiative | No automatic group. It's an ordinary combatant, unless you add it with the Groups and bands window. |
| Squad Combat Initiative is active | Minion! keeps its groups off and warns the GM. |
