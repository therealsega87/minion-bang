# What happens when

The odd cases, one line each.

## Attacks and damage

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| A minion is hit for 1 damage by an attack | It drops. |
| A minion passes its saving throw and takes half damage below its maximum | Nothing. No damage at all. |
| A minion passes its saving throw and the damage is at least its maximum | It drops. |
| A minion is immune to the damage | It takes none and stays up. |
| The attacker is a monster or an NPC | The Minion trait applies. There is no overkill. |
| The attack is a spell attack | The Minion trait applies. There is no overkill. |
| Several targets are hit by the same attack | Each target is handled on its own. The other targets are never offered as extra minions. |
| A minion already at 0 hit points is hit again | No new death is recorded. It can still start an overkill. |
| Someone edits a minion's hit points by hand | The Minion trait does not apply. |

## Overkill

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| The attacking player is offline | The GM gets the dialog. |
| The GM is looking at another scene | The hit minion drops. No overkill is offered, and the GM is told. |
| Nobody answers | The GM's waiting window stays open until someone clicks **Decide for them** or **Cancel overkill**. |
| An extra minion can't be downed | The others fall. The GM sees which one failed. |
| The player checks no minion and presses **Confirm** | No overkill. The chat says so. |

## Bands

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| A minion's challenge rating is missing | It is never grouped. |
| Only one minion of a kind is added before initiative | It stays alone and joins the next one of its kind. |
| The captain falls | The first member still standing becomes the captain. The band keeps its initiative. |
| A member rolls after the band rolled | The band's value does not change. |
| Initiative is reset | The bands are cleared. |
| Someone adds a minion to the combat after the initiative roll | It is a normal combatant. Bands are not changed after the roll. |
| Squad Combat Initiative is active | Minion! keeps its groups off and warns the GM. |
