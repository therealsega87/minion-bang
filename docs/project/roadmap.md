# Roadmap

Nothing here has a date.

## Next

- **Undo the last overkill.** A GM button revives the minions the last overkill downed, with their hit points, status and tracker mark restored.
- **Band status in the tracker.** A count next to each band showing how many members are still standing.
- **Newer dnd5e and Midi-QOL.** Minion! follows them once they are released and tested.

## Version 2.0

- **Bands of any size.** An **Add band** button in the Groups and bands window, bands of any size, and a warning when a size goes against the band size table.
- **Bands that merge.** At the start of a round, bands that lost members can merge into one band that acts on the lowest initiative of the bands.

## Later

- A view of the bands for players.
- Minions that take more than one hit.
- One saving throw for a whole band.

Have an idea? Open an [issue on GitHub](https://github.com/therealsega87/minion-bang/issues) or write on [Discord](https://discord.com/users/688483231531532323). The more people ask for something, the sooner it moves up.
