# FAQ

## Do I need the *Flee, Mortals!* module?

No. If it's installed, Minion! recognizes its minions automatically. You can [mark any other actor as a minion](../getting-started/marking-minions.md).

## Does it work without Midi-QOL?

No. Minion! works from the damage Midi-QOL calculates.

## Which Foundry versions does it support?

Foundry 13 and 14. See [Compatibility](../reference/compatibility.md) for the other requirements.

## Do my players have to do anything?

Only reload after an update. They attack as usual, and a player gets the overkill dialog when their character's attack earns one.

## Can a monster or an NPC cause overkill?

No. Only weapon attacks by player characters cause overkill. The [Minion trait](../table/minion-trait.md) applies to attacks from anyone.

## Does overkill work with spells?

No, only with weapon attacks.

## Why does the GM see a waiting window?

A player is deciding on an overkill. The GM can wait, decide for them or cancel it. If the player isn't connected, the GM gets the dialog directly.

## Can I put creatures that aren't minions in a band?

No. Bands are for minions only.

## Why won't it group my minions?

A minion needs a challenge rating to join a band, and automatic groups stop as soon as anyone in the combat rolls initiative. See [Setting up bands](../bands/setup.md#automatic-groups).

## Can I change the maximum band size?

Yes, for each challenge rating, in the [band size table](../reference/band-size.md). It changes the balance of your encounters.

## Can I make bands any size I want?

Not yet. See the [roadmap](../project/roadmap.md).

## Which languages does it support?

English and Italian. Foundry shows each user the language they picked.

## Where do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

On [GitHub](https://github.com/therealsega87/minion-bang/issues) or [Discord](https://discord.com/users/688483231531532323). See [Reporting a bug](bug-report.md).
