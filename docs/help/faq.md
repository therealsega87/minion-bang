# FAQ

## Do I need the *Flee, Mortals!* module?

No. If you have it, Minion! recognizes its minions on its own. Any other actor can be [marked as a minion](../getting-started/marking-minions.md).

## Does it work without Midi-QOL?

No. Minion! reads the damage Midi-QOL calculates.

## Which Foundry versions does it support?

Foundry 13 and 14. See [Compatibility](../reference/compatibility.md) for the other versions.

## Do the players need to do anything?

No. They attack as usual. A player sees the overkill dialog when their character's attack calls for one, and everyone reloads after an update.

## Can a monster or an NPC cause overkill?

No. Overkill follows weapon attacks made by player characters. The [Minion trait](../table/minion-trait.md) applies to attacks from anyone.

## Does overkill work with spells?

No, only with weapon attacks.

## Why does the GM see a waiting window?

A player is deciding on an overkill. The GM can wait, decide for them or cancel it. If the player isn't connected, the GM gets the dialog directly.

## Can I use bands for creatures that aren't minions?

No. Bands are for minions only.

## Why won't it group my minions?

A minion needs a challenge rating to be grouped. See [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md#the-groups-and-bands-button-doesnt-work).

## Can I change the maximum size of a band?

Yes, for each challenge rating, in the [band size table](../reference/band-size.md). Changing it changes the balance of your encounters.

## Can bands have any size I like?

Not yet. See the [roadmap](../project/roadmap.md).

## Is the interface translated?

English and Italian. Foundry uses the language each user has chosen.

## Where do I report a bug or ask for a feature?

On [GitHub](https://github.com/therealsega87/minion-bang/issues) or [Discord](https://discord.com/users/688483231531532323). See [Reporting a bug](bug-report.md).
